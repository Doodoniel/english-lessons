Hover over a phrase to see what it means. Watch the verb — it is the part people get wrong.

get get a degree · get a driving licence · get a job · get married
leave leave home · leave school
go / start go to university · start school
other be born · have children · move home · retire · vote
Careful: ❌ make a degree · ❌ go in university · ❌ marry with someone → get married to someone.

  • when I was five · I started school when I was five
  • two years ago · in 2019 · in the 1990s
  • before I'm 25 · by the time I'm 30
  • she's in her twenties / thirties
For Exercise 6: I want to + infinitive.
I want to leave home before I'm 25. ❌ I want to leaving

Reading strategy · p.21

  • Exercise 1 is a gist task — read fast, 60 seconds, just find who likes now and who likes the past. Don't stop at unknown words.
  • Exercise 2 is a detail task — this time find the exact line that proves your answer. Two of the three options are usually "almost right".
  • Exercise 3 — guess the meaning from the sentence around the word before you look it up.
Extreme adjectives. The article is full of them: huge = very big · tiny = very small · awful = very bad · exhausted = very tired. We don't say very huge — we say absolutely huge.

FSpeaking · the life interview 🎤 +30 XP

In pairs. Interview your partner about their life plan, then report back to the class about them, not about yourself.

  • Ask six questions using life events: At what age do you want to…?
  • Ask a follow-up for each one: Why? · Really? · Isn't that late/early?
  • Then tell the class three things: Marta wants to get a job before she goes to university because…
😈 Hard mode: your partner must not answer with a number only — every answer needs a reason.
