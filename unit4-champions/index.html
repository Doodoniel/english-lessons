Everything the warm-up game asks about is in this table. Keep it open while you play.

+er / +est		 fast → faster → the fastest

bad → worse → the worst
far → further → the furthest
Two classic mistakes: ❌ more faster · ❌ the most fastest. One form only! And after a comparative we use than: Rugby is harder than tennis.
Equal? as fast as · not as fast as. Squash is as fast as tennis.

do · go · play — the rule

dosports with no ball and no -ing name: do athletics · boxing · gymnastics · karate
goactivities ending in -ing: go climbing · cycling · ice skating · jogging · surfing · swimming · windsurfing · snowboarding
playgames with a ball / puck and an opponent: play ice hockey · rugby · squash · table tennis · tennis · volleyball · basketball
Careful: ❌ play athletics · ❌ do jogging · ❌ go tennis. And note: go swimming but play water polo.

Reading strategy · missing sentences (p.25 Ex.2)

  • Read the whole text first without the gaps — get the story.
  • Look at the words after the gap, not only before. These mean… → the missing sentence must contain a plural thing (gears!).
  • Hunt for reference words: This · These · They · She · It · either · too · also. They always point back to something.
  • Three sentences are extra. They usually repeat a word from the text to look attractive — check the logic, not the words.
  • Read your finished text once more. Does every pronoun have a partner?

FSpeaking · the sports debate 🎤 +30 XP

Two minutes each, no preparation. Take a card, defend it — even if you disagree.

  • “Football is the most boring sport on TV.”
  • “BMX is more dangerous than boxing, so it shouldn't be in schools.”
  • “Girls' teams should get as much money as boys' teams.”
  • “Video games are better exercise for your brain than sport.”
