⚡ Decision tree

  • 1. Is it a name? → usually no article (Mount Fuji, Oxford Street, Tesco) — but plural, of, Republic or Kingdom → the.
  • 2. Does the listener know which one? → the.
  • 3. Singular and countable, new information? → a/an.
  • 4. Plural or uncountable, meant in general? → no article.
Only one of its kind → the: the sun, the internet, the first time, the best.

NEW 3 · the for the only one, superlatives, species and inventions
  • Only one in our world: the sun · the moon · the sky · the sea · the environment · the internet · the police · the government
  • Superlatives and ordinals: the best day · the first time · the last bus · the only problem · the same address
  • A species or an invention as a class: The tiger is in danger. · The telephone was invented in 1876. (The plural works too: Tigers are in danger.)
  • Groups of people: the young · the unemployed · the French
NEW 4 · Geography — the map test
therivers, seas, oceans, deserts, mountain ranges, groups of islands, plural or “political” country names, regions with of:
the Thames · the Baltic Sea · the Sahara · the Alps · the Canaries · the Netherlands · the United Arab Emirates · the Middle East · the north of Spain
no articlecontinents, most countries, cities, single mountains, lakes, single islands, parks:
Europe · Japan · Lisbon · Mount Fuji · Lake Como · Sicily · Central Park
Odd ones worth memorising: the Hague · the Vatican · the Gambia — cities and countries that keep the.
NEW 5 · Fixed expressions — learn the whole phrase
no articlego to bed · be in bed · at home · at work · at school · in hospital (BrE) · in prison · at sea · by car / bus / train · on foot · at night · in fact · on holiday
thein the morning / afternoon / evening · in the end (finally) · at the end (of the film) · on the whole · in the country · on the left · play the piano
a / anhave a good time · in a hurry · as a rule · take a seat · have a look · once a week · what a pity
Two traps. in the end = в конце концов, but at the end of… = в конце чего-то.
British She is in hospital (as a patient); American English says in the hospital.
NEW 6 · Articles in a text: how English introduces things
Watch how the article changes as a thing becomes known:
I saw a man outside the shop. The man was carrying an old suitcase. The suitcase looked heavy.
  • The reader can also “already know” from the situation: Could you close the door? (this room has one door)
  • …or from what follows: the man who lives next door · the centre of Lisbon
  • Headlines and notes drop articles to save space: MAN BITES DOG · Meeting cancelled — see manager
This is exactly what the reading task below tests: every gap is decided by what came before it in the text.
